About me

The short version: I've shipped products where the stakes were physical, financial, and now, intelligent.

Ex civil-engineering BA, now AI product builder

Concrete to code.

I started my career on construction sites, translating messy on-ground reality into a workforce SaaS that got 90% adoption. That pattern — talk to the people closest to the problem, then build the smallest thing that actually fixes it — has carried me through fintech, into evaluating frontier LLMs, and now into building AI products myself.

Today I lead 0-to-1 product for an AI coaching platform: writing the specs, shaping the model behavior, and building the frontend alongside my engineers. I care most about products that quietly earn trust — whether that's a bank trusting a risk model or a user trusting an AI coach.
